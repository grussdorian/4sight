# Logistics and Bunker Fuel Exposure

Inbound materials move on the Taipei-Shanghai and Singapore-Rotterdam freight
lanes. Both are exposed to bunker fuel price volatility.

- The bunker fuel index baseline is roughly 30. A reading above 50 raises freight
  cost and transit risk to medium; above 65 is high; above 80 is critical.
- The Taipei lane is more fuel-exposed than the Singapore lane. When fuel spikes,
  shift volume to the Singapore lane where capacity allows.
- Fuel volatility propagates into every dependent freight lane and therefore into
  overall supply chain risk.
