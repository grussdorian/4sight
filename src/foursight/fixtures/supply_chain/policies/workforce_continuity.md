# Workforce Continuity Policy

Fab 17 depends on specialist personnel for continuity.

- Alice Chen is the lithography process lead and a single point of failure for
  yield tuning. Bob Taylor is the night-shift packaging supervisor.
- Effective staffing capacity at or above 80% is healthy. Below 80% is medium
  risk, below 60% high, below 40% critical for a single-owner role.
- Single-owner roles with no cross-trained backup amplify risk: their reduced
  availability should be scored higher than a comparable drop on a staffed team.
- Maintenance crew staffing below 60% risks unplanned equipment downtime.
