# Lithography Line Continuity

The lithography line patterns wafers and is the throughput bottleneck for Fab 17.
It depends on SUMCO silicon yield and on Alice Chen for calibration.

- A yield excursion or the loss of the process lead both reduce lithography
  output and compound when they occur together.
- Lithography sits on a shared dependency: both engineering operations and the
  packaging line draw on its output, so its risk reaches the root by multiple
  paths and must not be double-counted.
