# Wafer Yield Quality Policy

SUMCO is our sole-source supplier for specialty 300mm silicon substrate. Yield
rate is the percentage of wafers passing electrical test after lithography.

- Healthy yield is at or above 85%. Normal seasonal variation is +/- 3%.
- Yield between 60% and 70% indicates a process excursion and must be escalated
  to the lithography process lead within one shift.
- Yield below 60% is a high-severity event: it cascades directly into Fab 17
  output because there is no qualified second source for this substrate.
- Yield below 50% is critical. Halt wafer starts and convene the materials
  review board.

Because SUMCO is single-sourced, yield degradation has no redundancy buffer and
should always be treated more seriously than a comparable drop at a dual-sourced
supplier.
