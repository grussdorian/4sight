# Wafer Sourcing and Supplier Redundancy

Fab 17 procures 300mm wafers from three sources: TSMC (primary), GlobalFoundries
(secondary), and SUMCO (specialty substrate).

- TSMC and GlobalFoundries are dual-qualified, so a supply shortfall at one can
  be partially absorbed by the other. Supply below 80% of plan is a concern;
  below 70% is high severity; below 60% is critical.
- Maintaining at least two qualified wafer sources is the core mitigation against
  single-supplier disruption such as a Taiwan typhoon or export restriction.
- Redundancy reduces the effective risk of any single supplier's shortfall.
